Questions, answered.

Anything else, ask us in Discord or book a call with an engineer.

What counts as a merged change?

A pull request from Tare that lands in your default branch. If it's closed, or reverted within seven days, it isn't billed.

Which models can Tare use?

Claude, GPT, Gemini, Mistral and open models such as Qwen Coder, or your own endpoint. You can pin models per repository.

Where does my code run?

In isolated sandboxes on Tare cloud, or on self-hosted runners in your own cloud. Every attempt gets its own container and scoped secrets.

How does Tare pick the winning attempt?

Every attempt runs your tests, types, lint and a scope check. Among the ones that pass, Tare prefers the smallest diff and the fastest tests, and tells you why.

Can we cap what we spend?

Yes. Set a weekly cap per repository, and Tare stops starting new tasks before you reach it.

Does Tare train on our code?

No. Your code is used to run your tasks and nothing else, and sandboxes are destroyed when the run ends.
