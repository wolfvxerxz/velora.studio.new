Questions

How does Glint know why something moved?+

It lines up the price with everything that happened around it: large wallet trades, new pools and listings, unlocks, announcements and posts on X. The story is the events that line up with the move, in order.

Is it financial advice?+

No. Glint tells you what happened. What you do with it is up to you.

Which chains does it cover?+

Solana, Ethereum, Base and Arbitrum today, with more on the way.

Does it run on Windows?+

Glint is a native Mac app for now. A web version is coming, and it will share your watchlist and alerts.

What does Pro add?+

Alerts on causes, like a whale buying or a new listing, not just on prices. Plus a month of history on every story, and a watchlist that syncs across your Macs.
