Three things, done for you

  1. Book it once

    Add a client and their weekly slot. Kept repeats it every week, moves it when you drag it, and shows what each session counts toward.

  2. Every session punches the card

    Finish a session and its hole is punched. You always know how far a client is into the period, and what's left before you bill.

  3. Paid, without chasing

    When the card is full the invoice is ready. Kept nudges the client two days before it's due, and tells you the moment it's paid.
