Questions

What does Kept count?

Sessions. You decide how many make a billing period for each client, from one to twelve, and every finished session punches a hole in that client's card.

How do the reminders work?

When a card fills, Kept drafts the invoice for you to send. Two days before it's due, it nudges the client with a short, polite note you can edit.

Does it work with my calendar?

Kept reads Google and Apple calendars so sessions you already booked show up, and it keeps its own sessions in sync both ways.

Is my data private?

Your clients and invoices stay on your devices and in your iCloud. Kept never sees them.
