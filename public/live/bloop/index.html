Any questions?

How launching works, who sees what, and where your coins live.

What does it cost to launch a coin?+

Launching is free. You only pay the network fee on the chain you pick, shown before you press Launch.

Which chains does Bloop support?+

Base and Solana today. Every coin shows its chain on its face, so you always know where it lives.

Who can see my wallets?+

Only you. Friends see the coins you hold, never your balances, unless you choose to share them.

How do airdrops work?+

Coins can send their holders a gift. Each airdrop says why it's yours, and one tap claims it.

Is Bloop financial advice?+

No. Bloop shows what's happening and who holds what. What you do with it is up to you.
